A note on accuracy. We're grateful for the care most reporters take, and clear, accurate science communication matters to us. Even so, we can't control how every story or interview frames our research, and findings are occasionally simplified or overstated. We stand behind the claims in our peer-reviewed papers — not necessarily the wording of the coverage about them. For precise results and their limits, please return to the original paper (linked wherever available above) or contact its corresponding author.
